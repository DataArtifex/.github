## Organization & Project Vision

**Data Artifex** is a Python-based, open-source ecosystem designed to elevate data into API-powered, machine-actionable digital knowledge.

### Our Vision
- **Documentation**: Foster comprehensive data documentation accessible to both humans and machines.
- **API-First**: Facilitate rapid publication of data and metadata via APIs.
- **Intelligence**: Unleash data-driven machine intelligence.
- **Efficiency**: Significantly reduce time spent on data wrangling.
- **Standards**: Support and drive the adoption of industry standards and best practices.
- **Natural Language**: Enable natural language-driven data management.

### Technical Strategy
We aim to bridge the gap in traditional data publishing by promoting **metadata/API-first** practices. Our approach involves building a collection of specialized, well-orchestrated open-source packages powered by metadata standards, knowledge graphs, intelligent agents, and APIs. We focus particularly on **High-Value Datasets (HVDs)** to maximize societal and humanitarian impact.

### Core Standards & Specifications
We utilize across our tools:
- **Foundational**: JSON Schema, Semantic Web standards.
- **Documentation & Cataloging**: DDI, DCAT, schema.org.
- **Data Packages**: MLCommons Croissant, RO-Crates.
- **Governance**: ODRL.
